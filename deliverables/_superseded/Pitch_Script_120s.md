## Judge Q&A Defense Sheet

| Potential Judge Question | Grounded Evidence-Based Answer | Source Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **"Why won't store owners just use a calculator or WhatsApp?"** | Calculators leave no audit trail or suki balances; paper kwadernos tear and get misplaced. Chat apps require typing 10+ words per item. TindaWise takes 3 seconds of natural speech while hands are bagging goods. | Field interview themes (Ate Mina, Kuya Ronny); `sarisaristore_interview.md` |
| **"What happens when the internet drops?"** | TindaWise is offline-first. Our local rule-based Taglish entity parser executes directly on device in ~1.4 ms using local regex/catalog patterns. No internet is required to record utang. | `src/frontend/index.html` (`extractTaglishUtang`); `src/backend/ledger_engine.py` |
| **"Can the AI hallucinate prices or overcharge customers?"** | Never. We maintain strict financial boundaries. The AI only outputs extracted text strings (debtor name, SKU name, quantity). Deterministic arithmetic multiplies quantity by the storekeeper's verified catalog price. | Slide 4 Architecture; `DEFAULT_PRICE_LIST` in `ledger_engine.py` |
| **"Does sending SMS violate Data Privacy (RA 10173) or spam laws?"** | No. TindaWise uses standard Android `smsto:` device intents. The store owner manually taps "Send SMS" in their native messaging app. No customer numbers are transmitted to external servers. | `src/frontend/index.html` (`sendSmsReminder`); RA 10173 compliance |
| **"Why not charge stores immediately?"** | Sari-sari stores operate on thin daily margins. Prematurely paywalling counter logging kills organic adoption. We prove daily utility first, then validate willingness to pay for optional backup and supplier intelligence. | Slide 7 Business Model; `sarisaristore_interview.md` Implication #7 |
