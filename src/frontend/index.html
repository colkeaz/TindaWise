🤖 TindaWise AI Architecture & Settings

Where is the AI in TindaWise?
  1. Layer 1 (Perception): Speech-to-Text acoustic modeling (Taglish voice capture via Android RecognizerIntent).
  2. Layer 2 (Semantic Extraction — Core AI): Natural language entity extraction (debtor, due date, fractional quantities e.g. kalahati = 0.5, and fuzzy product SKU mapping).
  3. Financial Safety Guardrail: AI NEVER guesses money. Unit prices come from your Owner Price List and are summed in plain deterministic code.
  4. Layer 3 (Generative Social Reminder): Contextual polite Tagalog reminder drafting for manual sending.
Key is saved only locally in your browser storage. If offline or invalid, auto-falls back to on-device NLP.

RA 10173 Data Privacy Compliance

  • Push-to-Talk & 0-Day Audio Retention: Audio is captured only when you tap Speak and is discarded immediately after speech-to-text transcription. No voice recordings are saved to storage.
  • Local-First Ledger Storage: Customer nicknames and balances are saved locally on your phone. No government IDs are collected.
  • Deterministic Math: AI never sets prices or changes balances without your explicit confirmation.
  • Manual SMS Sending: Reminders are drafted on your phone and sent manually by you via SMS or Messenger.
